Depositions, client intake calls, witness interviews — recordings that come up in legal work are frequently privileged, and every cloud transcription service means handing that recording to a third party. This doesn't. A working version of this already existed as a personal script wrapped in a macOS droplet; this project gives it a real interface and makes it something other people can actually clone and run, not just read.
